How NEXA works

Pi-for-Pi lending.
The protocol never touches USD.

Borrowers pledge locked Pi as collateral and receive unlocked Pi as the loan principal. They sell the loan Pi for cash on their existing CEX accounts. At unlock, the smart contract auto-settles in Pi. No oracle, no stablecoin, no cross-chain settlement.

1

Pledge locked Pi

Use your existing locked Pi positions as collateral. Mining bonus continues on whichever side of the architecture preserves it.

→
2

Receive unlocked Pi

The contract sends unlocked Pi (the loan principal) directly to your wallet. Sell it for cash on Kraken, OKX, or any CEX where you already have an account.

→
3

Auto-settle at unlock

When your collateral lockup expires, the contract receives the unlocking Pi, pays the lender what's owed, and returns any surplus to your wallet automatically.

WHY THIS WORKS

Every prior on-chain credit attempt (Maple, TrueFi, Goldfinch) collapsed scoring and lending into one protocol — defaults destroyed TVL. NEXA's structural insight is that when collateral and debt are the same asset, default risk is eliminated. The Pi unlocks regardless of price; the contract gets it; the lender gets paid.

How we de-risk this

Trust earned by design, not promised.

Every architectural decision in NEXA is downstream of one principle: be honest about the risks, design around them, ship code others can audit.

⌬

Stellar Soroban inheritance

Built on Pi's Soroban runtime — Stellar's production smart-contract platform with $100M of Soroban Adoption Fund deployment and multiple top-tier audits behind it.

◈

Audit-first roadmap

$100K of our seed earmarked for top-tier audit (Trail of Bits / OpenZeppelin / Spearbit) before mainnet launch. No code touches user funds without an independent review.

●

Identity by Pi's KYC

Every NEXA user is already identity-verified by Pi Network's mandatory KYC. No new accounts, no anonymous actors. Sybil-resistance is structural, not bandaid.

Same-asset collateral

Pi-denominated loans backed by locked Pi mean collateral and debt move together. The structural failure mode that killed Maple and TrueFi does not exist here.

Those protocols failed because of default risk — they lent stablecoins to whitelisted institutions who could (and did) default. NEXA's design eliminates default risk structurally: we don't lend USD against Pi. We lend Pi against locked Pi. Collateral and debt are the same asset. There is no scenario where a borrower can "walk away" with the lender's funds, because the contract holds the collateral until settlement.
